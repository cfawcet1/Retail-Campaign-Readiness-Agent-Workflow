# Launch Notes: Soft Lounge Spring Drop
**Launch Date:** August 22, 2026

## Overview
Soft Lounge Spring Drop is a 5-SKU extension of our core Soft Lounge franchise, introducing two new colorways (sienna and dusty rose) alongside restocked core styles in onyx and marble. This is a full channel launch — DTC, email, SMS, and paid social — targeting our existing Soft Lounge customer base as well as new customer acquisition via influencer seeding.
